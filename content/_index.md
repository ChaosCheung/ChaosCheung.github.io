            <h3>Applied cryptography</h3>

It applies threshold cryptography, digital signatures, zero-knowledge proofs, and authenticated broadcast encryption to deliver rigorous security with practical efficiency. </p>

          <article class="research-card">
            <span class="research-index">02</span>
            <h3>Privacy preservation</h3>
            <p>My research develops privacy-preserving mechanisms that protect users' identities, access patterns, and sensitive transaction data. It balances anonymity and unlinkability with accountability, public verifiability, fine-grained access control, and regulatory traceability.</p>
          </article>
          <article class="research-card">
            <span class="research-index">03</span>
            <h3>Distributed network security</h3>
            <p>My research designs secure protocols for distributed and decentralized systems, including decentralized finance, blockchain-enabled smart grids, and IoT communication. It addresses malicious participants and data leakage while maintaining verifiability and practical efficiency.</p>
